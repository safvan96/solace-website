!WARNING · HUB PvP

Start a fight in town and lose, and your life is forfeit.

PvP is allowed in the Hub; raids are not. A winning attacker may only injure. A losing attacker falls under NVL: the person they attacked may kill them, take them as a slave, or have them jailed in the Hub.

Our promise

A lively world is not the same as a world that revolves around one faction. Nobles, wanderers, craftspeople, merchants and troublemakers should all find reasons to log in.

Your character can face consequences without your place in the community becoming disposable.

What SOLACE is not

  • Not a mandatory kingdom storyline.
  • Not dice-based PvP.
  • Not a permanent safe zone.
  • Not a place where a staff member's character decides an OOC dispute involving their own group.

IF YOU START PvP IN THE HUB
OUTCOME A
Attacker wins
  • May give the victim Injury only.
  • No robbery. No kidnapping.
  • Regular RP and combat rules apply otherwise.
!OUTCOME B · DANGER
Attacker loses: NVL
  • The defeated attacker falls under No Value of Life (NVL).
  • The person they attacked may kill them, take them as a slave or have them jailed in the Hub.
  • Enslavement still follows the consent rules. If the attacker refuses enslavement, jail in the Hub is the alternative.

NVL applies only to the attacker who started the fight and lost. Hub raids and sieges are never allowed.
