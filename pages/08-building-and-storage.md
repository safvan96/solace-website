# Building, Clans & Storage
*Build a believable place people can actually visit.*

## Main building rules
- Every player, including a solo player, must use a clan for tracked building and storage. Maximum inherited clan size: **12 players**.
- A clan may have **one permanent base**. Temporary small RP camps/outposts must be removed after use unless staff grants extra time. No second permanent storage base.
- Maximum height without permission: **10 wall heights**. No foundation spam: normally up to **2 foundation layers** for uneven terrain, at most **3** where genuinely needed; use walls/pillars otherwise.
- No honeycombing, empty-room door mazes, thermal-effect trap spam, impossible floating structures or other raid-resistance exploits.
- Cliff and raised bases need believable non-climbing access such as stairs; islands need a bridge, unless a specifically approved technical concept applies. Underwater/elevator-only sections cannot house otherwise unreachable protected storage or be exploited during conflict.
- Do not block main routes without an accessible alternate passage. Do not build outside the green wall, under the mesh or in instanced dungeons. Keep marked POIs and protected zones clear.
- Large clans (**5+ members**) cannot build within **3/4 map grid** of another large base, and no more than **three clans** may cluster within that radius. Sister-clanning (multiple clans acting as one indivisible group to bypass limits) is prohibited; IC vassalage may be allowed without evading spacing rules.
- Clan warps must be placed at least **1/2 map grid** from base locations and need the applicable staff assistance. Protected-zone buffer rules still apply.
- Inactivity: a member who has not logged in for **3 weeks** is removed from the clan under SOLACE's chosen replacement for the former two-week deletion. Inactive members do not count for inflation of building/decoration allowances in audits; notify staff via ticket for absence.

## Mount and decorative NPC allowances
Maximum **one mount per player**, with a clan cap of **three**. Flaggis and wallpapers need staff ticket approval and should remain under the admin-controlled placement workflow. Limits:

| Active clan size | Flaggis | Wallpapers |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1–4 | 3 | 1 |
| 5–8 | 6 | 2 |
| 9–12 | 9 | 3 |

One **Thespian per clan**, only in the **Hub Player Market**. Turn off head tracking; use vanilla armour only and no accessories where this applies to your installed NPC mod. No NPC as unraidable gold/item storage, and no trading of items intended to be non-droppable.

## Storage restrictions
The legacy rule limits inventories for performance. Do not use decorative containers as hidden storage. Inherited personal inventory cap is **30 slots excluding the action bar**, subject to actual deployed mod configuration and approved in-world expansion. A standard personal storage chest has **25 slots**; an improved version through Masonry has **40**. The legacy per-base inventory caps are:

## Restricted locations
**No fictional coordinates are published.** Special utility sites often reserve a **1/2-grid** perimeter; principal public market/arena-like sites a **3/4-grid** perimeter. A SOLACE restricted-map overlay will be published after the map is confirmed. All instanced dungeons remain no-build zones.
