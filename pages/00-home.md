# SOLACE
**21+ · Conan Exiles · RP / PvP / PvE**

## An exile is more than their sentence.
Build a home. Find your people. Start something worth remembering.

SOLACE is an English-language Conan roleplay community for players who want more than a race to the strongest weapon. A lonely camp can become a trading post. An argument can start a feud. A lost fight can become the beginning of a better story. Spend your evening in the tavern, hunt a bounty, set up a clan, study forbidden magic or simply make a life for your character.

**Start your story** → `/start`  |  **Read the rules** → `/rules`

### Find your place
- **The World**: Conan-inspired, lore-conscious stories with space for both quiet nights and real consequences.
- **Eight Professions**: Bard, Bounty Hunter, Medic, Fighter, Thief, Alchemist, Slaver and Courtier.
- **Three Special Classes**: Sorcerer, Blacksmith and Wise, with quest- and ritual-based development.
- **Mechanical PvP**: Skill-based combat, with clear intent and post-fight consequences.
- **The Hub**: Shops, services, work, stories and a strict warning for those who start trouble inside town.
- **Living Economy**: Trade, crafting, daily silver and weekly gold.

## Our promise
A lively world is not the same as a world that revolves around one faction. Nobles, wanderers, craftspeople, merchants and troublemakers should all find reasons to log in. Your character can face consequences without your place in the community becoming disposable.

### What SOLACE is not
Not a mandatory kingdom storyline. Not dice-based PvP. Not a permanent safe zone. Not a place where a staff member's character decides an OOC dispute involving their own group.
