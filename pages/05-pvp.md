# PvP Rules
**Mechanical PvP is the default combat resolution. SOLACE does not use dice PvP.**

## The 30-second answer
An aggressor declares an RP intent and the server's PvP-intent command. Everyone within the applicable local range must make their initial **Join** or **Surrender** response. Once all affected people have declared **Ready**, the initiating player starts the countdown. You cannot summon late allies into an already active fight. There is one home-base defense exception detailed below.

## 1. Where PvP is possible
Your character is IC and PvP can occur throughout the map, except the designated starter zone and other expressly published protected technical locations. **The Hub permits PvP but applies its own sharply restricted outcomes.** Read `/hub` before you initiate there.

## 2. Exact initiation sequence
1. Give a clear IC emote explaining the threatening act and write `(PvP Intent)`; issue the approved server command (legacy example: `/pvpintent`, subject to bot implementation).
2. Everyone affected stays stationary during declaration, except where a published character merit expressly permits a limited move. Any such movement is declared with its merit.
3. Everyone within the intent's local range responds with a brief emote and **Join** (`/pvpjoin`) or **Surrender** (`/pvpsurrender`). Join includes the right to flee once the fight begins; Surrender means no mechanical fight and the character enters the applicable consequence stage.
4. Players confirm `Ready` or `R` when declarations have finished.
5. Initiator runs `/pvp start` to begin the **10-second** start timer. Fight only after the countdown ends. [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: which installed plugin provides these exact commands; do not show nonfunctional slash commands as live instructions.]

## 3. Nearby people and late joining
A bystander within the original initiation range must respond to the initial intent. **After combat becomes active, no new participant may join from outside.** The sole exception: if the character who was attacked successfully reaches **their own base**, characters already **inside that base** may take part in defending them. They may not teleport to create artificial reinforcements. [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: what counts as 'inside', and how staff records the exception.]

## 4. Winning, losing, surrendering
After being mechanically downed, do not rejoin the battle. Return promptly to the down location if the game's death/respawn system requires it, kneel or lie there and apply the configured injured status. Downed players cannot continue combat. The standard consequence phase follows unless a specific zone overrides it. Leaving a downed player without RP for **30 minutes** after PvP ends permits them to leave still injured; for battles of **10+ players**, that unattended limit is **45 minutes**.

## 5. Teleport, mounts, terrain and movement
- No portals, wayshrines or warps in the approach to an active fight, in the fight or during consequences, save the permitted technical return to a downed body. You cannot initiate/join a fight for **15 minutes** after a warp.
- No mounted combat. Dismount immediately if intent is made, and put your mount on guard.
- A fleeing player must evade active pursuit for **10 minutes** before the fight ends. A new encounter afterwards requires a fresh initiation. Having eluded pursuit does not grant an instant return warp; reach your base by normal travel where the inherited rule requires it.
- Water cannot be looped for healing or damage avoidance. Pursuers may attack a fleeing target in water; a fleeing player may not turn the water into an attack exploit. Cross by the shortest sensible route and do not repeatedly re-enter the same water source.
- Do not change locks, relocate decoration, dismantle or exploit building pieces after intent/raid initiation. Lock changes outside combat must be clearly emoted and interruptible.
- Height sliders: in the legacy mechanical-PvP accommodation, a character shorter than **171** adjusts to **171–189** after initiation and restores their RP appearance afterwards. [CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: mod/slider scale.]
- No spawn/warp camping. Stay at least **20 foundations** from public spawns/warps when patrolling and don't linger to stage an ambush.
- If flying is available through a mod, land within **4 foundations** of an active intent and do not use flight to initiate an ambush.

## 6. Abilities, dungeons, combat effects
An aggressive spell/merit/ability used around others must be properly emoted to give them a chance to respond; the recipient may treat it as intent. Mechanical spells/abilities are allowed during active PvP and in PvE where they do not affect other players. On leaving an instance during an active fight, move at least **5 foundations** clear of the entrance before attacking; do not use the loading zone to heal/camp cooldowns. A fleeing participant cannot return to the same instance for **30 minutes** or until the fight has ended, whichever inherited restrictions apply. Where a public event is formally tagged **KOS**, follow the event notice and record your join; *there is no blanket KOS permission outside advertised KOS events.*

## 7. After the fight
Regular world encounters use `/rules/consequences`. The Hub has different and more severe rules **for the aggressor who loses**, and only Injury is permitted as a winning aggressor's consequence in the Hub. Raid battles additionally use `/rules/raids`.

## Example: a chase back home
A is initiated on the road. A joins and flees; the fight has already started. A reaches their **own base**. Only defenders already inside that base may now enter under SOLACE's narrowly defined exception. Friends who receive a Discord message, spawn elsewhere or warp across the map may not join.
