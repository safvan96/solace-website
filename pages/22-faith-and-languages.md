# Languages
*Which tongues your character speaks.*

## Languages

### How languages work
- Everyone speaks **Trader's Tongue**, the common language of markets, roads and ports across the Exiled Lands.
- Your **race** gives you two more languages automatically: your **native tongue** at full fluency, and the tongue of the **people you grew up beside**: the neighbours, rulers or trading partners your people deal with every day.
- Languages cannot be picked freely. You can **learn** other languages in play: hearing a language spoken around you slowly teaches it to you over time.
- Choose the language you speak in from the language menu in chat. Characters who do not know that language will not understand you.
- If your character changes race, the old race's languages are removed and the new ones are given.
