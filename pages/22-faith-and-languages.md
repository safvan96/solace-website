# Languages
*Every exile arrives with only two tongues: the one they were born to, and the Trader's Tongue spoken in every market of the Exiled Lands. Everything else is learned.*

## How languages work
- Your character starts **fluent** in their **native tongue** (set by race) and in **Trader's Tongue**.
- Any other language must be **taught in roleplay** by a character who speaks it fluently.
- A teacher uses the **Teach** ability on the student (no roll, within 6 tiles). Each teacher can use each Teach ability **once every 24 hours**.
- Each lesson raises the student's knowledge by **10%**: 5 lessons = 50%, **10 lessons = fluent**.
- A student can take **one lesson per language every 5 days**. Learning a tongue properly takes time.
- Once fluent, you receive **Fluent: (language)** and can teach it yourself.
- Lessons never lower a language you already speak fluently. Changing race resets your starting languages.
- Choose the language you speak in from the language menu in chat. Characters who do not know that language will not understand you.
