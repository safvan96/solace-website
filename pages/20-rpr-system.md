# Dice & RPR System

*Roleplay Rolls, Profession Mastery & Class Ability Checks.*

*SOLACE uses a lightweight d20 roleplay system for uncertain RP actions, Profession abilities, Class abilities, investigations and opposed roleplay actions. Mechanical PvP remains Conan Exiles mechanical combat and is not replaced by dice.*

---

## 1. The Core Rule

Most RPR checks use:

**1d20 + Final Skill Modifier**

Your Final Skill Modifier is built from three possible sources:

1. **Category Bonus**
2. **Allocated Skill Points**
3. **Profession / Class Bonus**

### Character Creation

Every character receives:

- **5 Category Points**
- **35 Skill Points**

### Category Point Rules

- The 5 Category Points are distributed between the four main categories.
- A single category may receive a maximum of **+3**.
- Every point placed into a category gives **+1 to every skill inside that category**.

### Skill Point Rules

- The player distributes **35 Skill Points** between the 16 individual RPR skills.
- A single skill may receive a maximum of **5 allocated Skill Points** during character creation.

### Profession and Class Bonuses

- Every Profession grants **+2 to two RPR skills** that represent its specialization.
- Every Class grants **+2 to two RPR skills** that represent its specialization.
- Profession and Class bonuses stack with Category and allocated Skill Points.
- A skill's **final effective modifier can never exceed +10**.

### Example

A Courtier has:

- Social +2
- Persuasion +4 from allocated Skill Points
- Courtier Profession Bonus: Persuasion +2

Their final Persuasion modifier is:

**+8**

If Silver Tongue requires 16+, the player rolls:

**1d20 + 8**

A natural roll of 8 gives a final result of 16 and succeeds.

## 2. The Four Main Categories

### PHYSICAL

Physical represents bodily control, movement, coordination and direct physical action.

#### Athletics
Used for raw physical effort.

Examples:
- Climbing a difficult surface.
- Swimming through dangerous water.
- Forcing open a stuck object.
- Lifting or dragging something heavy.
- Holding someone back through physical strength when the scene allows an RPR check.

#### Acrobatics
Used for balance, controlled movement and difficult physical positioning.

Examples:
- Keeping balance on a narrow ledge.
- Landing safely after a difficult jump.
- Moving across unstable terrain.
- Escaping a physical hold when the scene is being resolved through RPR rather than mechanical PvP.

#### Sleight of Hand
Used for precise hand movements and subtle manual actions.

Examples:
- Picking a lock.
- Concealing a small object.
- Quietly moving or taking a small item during RP.
- Performing a delicate hand-based trick.

#### Stealth
Used to move, hide or remain unnoticed.

Examples:
- Moving quietly through a settlement.
- Hiding from someone searching nearby.
- Approaching a location without drawing attention.

**Stealth does not create magical invisibility and does not override what another character could clearly see in game.**

---

### KNOWLEDGE

Knowledge represents education, learned information, research and technical understanding.

#### Arcana
Used for knowledge of sorcery, unnatural phenomena, rituals and supernatural signs.

Examples:
- Recognizing signs of sorcery.
- Studying the remains of a ritual.
- Understanding whether an occult symbol is meaningful.
- Identifying unusual magical behavior when the character could reasonably know it.

#### History
Used for historical and cultural knowledge.

Examples:
- Remembering an old war or ruler.
- Recognizing a historical banner.
- Knowing something about an ancient settlement.
- Recalling important cultural events.

#### Investigation
Used for deliberate, detailed examination and connecting evidence.

Examples:
- Examining a murder scene.
- Searching a room for hidden clues.
- Studying a damaged weapon to understand what happened.
- Comparing documents or physical evidence.

Investigation is normally used when the character is **actively examining something**, while Perception is normally used to **notice something**.

#### Religion
Used for gods, religious customs, sacred symbols and rituals.

Examples:
- Recognizing a religious symbol.
- Understanding the purpose of a shrine.
- Recalling the customs of a faith.
- Performing or interpreting religious rites when appropriate.

#### Medicine
Used for bodily injuries, illness, poison and medical understanding.

Examples:
- Examining a wound.
- Determining whether someone was poisoned.
- Understanding the likely cause of an injury.
- Assessing whether a wounded person is stable.

Medicine checks do not replace the special healing abilities of the Medic Profession.

---

### INSTINCT

Instinct represents awareness, intuition, survival sense and reading the world around the character.

#### Perception
Used to notice details through sight, hearing and general awareness.

Examples:
- Hearing movement nearby.
- Spotting fresh blood on the ground.
- Noticing someone attempting to hide.
- Seeing a detail that is not automatically obvious.

#### Insight
Used to read behavior, intent, emotional cues and inconsistencies.

Examples:
- Feeling that someone is hiding something.
- Reading a person's mood.
- Detecting suspicious behavior.
- Opposing a Deception roll.

A normal Insight check does **not** force another player to reveal the full truth. Special Profession or Class abilities may create stronger effects when their rules explicitly say so.

#### Survival
Used for tracking, navigation and wilderness knowledge.

Examples:
- Following tracks.
- Determining where a trail is heading.
- Navigating through difficult terrain.
- Recognizing signs that someone recently passed through an area.


---

### SOCIAL

Social represents communication, manipulation, pressure and performance.

#### Persuasion
Used for honest persuasion, diplomacy and negotiation.

Examples:
- Convincing a guard to hear you out.
- Negotiating a better agreement.
- Trying to calm a disagreement through words.

A normal Persuasion roll does not remove another player's agency. It helps resolve uncertain social RP but cannot force consent-based actions unless a specific Profession or Class ability explicitly allows an effect under its own rules.

#### Deception
Used for lies, false stories, disguises and misleading information.

Examples:
- Lying about where you were.
- Pretending to represent someone else.
- Giving a believable false explanation.

A common opposed check is:

**Deception vs Insight**

#### Intimidation
Used for threats, fear and social pressure.

Examples:
- Threatening someone into backing away.
- Establishing a frightening presence.
- Trying to make someone hesitate through fear.

Normal Intimidation does not automatically force another player to obey. Profession abilities such as Slaver Intimidation use their own defined mechanical effect.

#### Performance
Used for music, storytelling, acting, speeches and entertainment.

Examples:
- Playing music in a tavern.
- Giving a public speech.
- Telling a story to hold a crowd's attention.
- Performing at an event.

## 3. General RP Checks

RPR is not only for Profession or Class abilities. Any player may use the system when an uncertain RP action needs a fair resolution.

### Standard Difficulty Guide

Staff, event runners or the players involved may use the following difficulty guide:

| Difficulty | Required Result |
|---|---:|
| Simple | **8+** |
| Standard | **12+** |
| Difficult | **16+** |
| Extreme | **20+** |

The final result is always:

**1d20 + Final Skill Modifier**

#### Example: Investigation

A player examines a body for details that are not immediately obvious.

The scene runner sets the check at **16**.

The character has Investigation +5.

They roll 13.

**13 + 5 = 18, Success.**

The scene runner provides the clue that could reasonably be discovered.

#### Example: Perception

A player wants to notice whether someone recently passed through a dark alley.

The check is **12**.

The character has Perception +3 and rolls 7.

**7 + 3 = 10, Failure.**

They do not notice anything reliable.

## 4. Opposed RP Checks

When two characters are actively opposing each other, both may roll.

The higher final result wins.

Common examples:

- **Deception vs Insight**
- **Stealth vs Perception**
- **Sleight of Hand vs Perception**
- **Athletics vs Athletics**
- **Athletics vs Acrobatics**, where appropriate

#### Example: Lie vs Insight

Character A tells a deliberate lie.

- Character A rolls **1d20 + Deception**.
- Character B rolls **1d20 + Insight**.

Character A: 14 + 5 = **19**

Character B: 12 + 4 = **16**

Character A wins the opposed check.

Character B does not gain mechanical confirmation that the statement is false.

#### Tie

If an opposed check ends in a tie, the existing situation remains unchanged unless both players agree on another outcome.

## 5. RPR Quality Rule

RPR exists to support roleplay, not replace it.

A roll must be supported by an appropriate RP attempt.

Players may not repeatedly make one-line emotes, spam rolls or arrange empty scenes only to gain progression, Profession Mastery or mechanical advantages.

Qualifying RP should be:

- Properly roleplayed.
- Appropriate to the character and situation.
- Given enough time and effort to create an actual scene.
- Supported by believable context.
- Meaningful enough to justify the requested result.

Staff may reject progression evidence that exists only to farm counters or rewards.

**Progression requirements are story milestones, not achievement farming.**

## 10. Important Limits

- RPR does not replace mechanical PvP.
- A successful normal RP roll does not automatically remove another player's agency.
- Consent rules still apply to all consent-sensitive content.
- A Profession/Class ability only creates the special effect written in that ability's rules.
- Dice do not create information that could not reasonably exist in the scene.
- Staff may reduce, deny or contextualize information when a roll is technically high but the RP provides no believable basis for the requested result.
- Profession Mastery evidence must show real, meaningful scenes rather than mechanical farming.
- `/actname`, disguises and identity-hiding mechanics are not bypassed by Famous Bard or Unstoppable reputation recognition.
