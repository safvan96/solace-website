# Economy, Crafting & Quests
*A coin is more interesting when it changes hands.*

## Currency
All payments in SOLACE use **Silver Coins** and **Gold Coins**: Hub Duty pays 5 Silver, the Gem Hunt 5 Silver, the Weekly Hunt 1 Gold, and a caravan ride costs 1 Silver.

| Icon | Item | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| ![Solace Silver Coin](img/items/Solace_Silver_Coin.png) | **Solace Silver Coin** | Everyday money: Hub Duty pay, caravan fares, the Daily Hunt and most trade between players. |
| ![Solace Gold Coin](img/items/Solace_Gold_Coin.png) | **Solace Gold Coin** | Big rewards and big purchases: the Weekly Hunt, jewels and blacksmith work. |

## Gem Hunt and Weekly Hunt
**Thaddeus, the Scholar** pays the hunters who keep the Exiled Lands in check. Talk to Thaddeus in the Hub to receive both hunts in your Quest Log.

| Hunt | Where | What to bring | Reward | How often |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Gem Hunt: The Mounds of the Dead** | The Mounds of the Dead: barrows older than any kingdom, where a skull-faced army of the dead has begun to walk again, led by the Creature of the Mounds | **5 Green Gems** | **5 Silver Coins** | once per day |
| **Weekly Hunt: The Abandoned Silvermine** | The Abandoned Silvermine: emptied of miners long ago and claimed by creatures and darker things; the Guardians stalk its deep shafts | **30 Blue Gems** | **1 Gold Coin** | once per week |

| Icon | Item | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| ![Green Gem](img/items/Green_Gem.png) | **Green Gem** | Cut from the creatures of the Mounds of the Dead. Stackable. Bring 5 to Thaddeus for 5 Silver (once a day). |
| ![Blue Gem](img/items/Blue_Gem.png) | **Blue Gem** | Carried by the guardians of the Abandoned Silvermine. Stackable. Bring 30 to Thaddeus for 1 Gold (once a week). |

- The bosses drop their gems **on their corpse**. Whoever takes them first owns them; protect your kill or fight for it.
- Your Quest Log counts the gems you carry (for example 3/5). When it is complete, return to Thaddeus.
- Thaddeus takes exactly 5 (or 30) gems; any extra gems stay with you.

## Hub Duty
**Hub Duty** is one daily quest: a **30-minute** shift in the Hub pays **5 Silver**. One Hub Duty per character every 24 hours (guard **or** tavern, not both).

### City Guard Duty: Fighter, Slaver, Thief
The Town Guard has not slept in days and asks you to cover their post. Stay inside the guard post for 30 minutes.

### Tavern Work: Bard, Bounty Hunter, Medic, Alchemist, Courtier
A caravan has arrived and the Tavern Keeper needs help. Stay inside the tavern and the public bathhouse next door for 30 minutes.

### During the shift
- While working you are **On Duty** (shown above your character).
- Leaving the duty area ends the shift without pay. You may start again later the same day; only a completed shift counts as your daily Hub Duty.
- Roleplay during the shift is expected (staff discretion).

### Quest Log
When you start a shift, the quest appears in your **Quest Log** (Sudo menu → QuestLog → Active):
- **Hub Duty: Cover the Town Guard**: "Stand the watch for 30 minutes without leaving the guard post."
- **Hub Duty: The Tavern Rush**: "Look after the tavern and the bathhouse for 30 minutes without leaving."

A completed shift moves to **Completed**. Leaving the duty area removes the quest from your log.

### Medic Work
Medics may also earn money by offering healing services through RP.

Before starting treatment, the Medic must clearly state the price of the service to the patient.
- The price must be agreed, or at least clearly stated, before the healing RP begins.
- Once the treatment has been completed, the Medic may not introduce a new price afterward.
- A Medic cannot finish the healing first and then demand payment that was never mentioned beforehand.

## Jewels
Jewels strengthen the one who carries them. Carry a jewel in your inventory and its bonus shows in your character stats. There are four kinds, each in three tiers; a higher tier gives a bigger bonus. You can carry **one jewel of each kind** at the same time: one Strength, one Agility, one Health and one Stamina jewel, of any tier.

## Hub Market
See `/items` for every item with its icon.

The Hub sells several special items directly.

| Item | Effect | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Amulet of Solace** | +15 Strength, +15 Agility | 10 Gold |
| **Talisman of Solace** | +40 Health, +40 Stamina | 10 Gold |
| **Feast Ring** | Use/carry up to 1,000 Feast items without weight burden. Breaks after 1,000 uses. | 1 Gold |
| **Legendary Weapon Fitting** | +9 damage, +15 armour penetration. | 1 Gold |
| **Legendary Spike Weapon Fitting** | +15 armour penetration. | 1 Gold |
| **Legendary Armor Fitting** | +45 armour, +3 weight. | 1 Gold |

All player markets belong in the **Hub**. Market Thespians are limited to **one per clan**, and cannot be used as invulnerable banks or forbidden-item exchanges. Social trade can still take place between characters, but permanent player market placements are Hub-only.

### Feast Ring
The Feast Ring allows its wearer to use up to 1,000 Feast items without carrying their normal weight.
- **Durability:** 1,000 uses. After 1,000 uses, the ring breaks.
- **Hub price:** 1 Gold
- Can also be crafted by a Blacksmith.

### Legendary Weapon Fitting
A high-grade fitting designed to increase a weapon's overall offensive power.
- +9 damage
- +15 armour penetration
- **Hub price:** 1 Gold

### Legendary Spike Weapon Fitting
A specialized fitting designed to improve a weapon's ability to penetrate armor.
- +15 armour penetration
- **Hub price:** 1 Gold

### Legendary Armor Fitting
A reinforced fitting designed to increase the defensive value of armor.
- +45 armour, +3 weight
- **Hub price:** 1 Gold

### Amulet of Solace
A powerful Hub-exclusive accessory.
- +15 Strength
- +15 Agility
- **Hub price:** 10 Gold

### Talisman of Solace
A powerful Hub-exclusive accessory focused on survivability.
- +40 Health
- +40 Stamina
- **Hub price:** 10 Gold

## Farming philosophy
Routine Conan material farming should leave enough time for character-driven RP. The meetings discussed easier access to early build resources. This is a direction, **not** a promised server multiplier or a promise of endless free resources.
