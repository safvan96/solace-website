# Culture Dossiers
*Practical guidance for making a Conan-rooted character. These are cultural snapshots, not inherited stats or personality tests.*

This page expands the quick `/races` table. **Nothing here automatically grants a combat modifier, predetermined faction, 'enemy' label, costume requirement or permission to attack.** Appearance is varied within every people and the setting's chronology is not locked yet.

## Aquilonian
**Region:** Western Hyborian kingdoms. **Background:** A realm with frontier armies, trade routes and competing noble interests. **RP traits to explore:** civic loyalty, court service, village pragmatism, life on contested borders. **Potential tensions:** Nemedia's competing power, Pictish frontiers, northern Cimmerian border history. **Possible opening:** An ex-soldier sells protection to a caravan whose owner is Nemedian.

## Nemedian
**Region:** Eastern neighbor and rival of Aquilonia. **Background:** Influential kingdom with notable learning, institutions and political ambitions. **RP traits to explore:** scholarship, diplomacy, soldiering, personal status. **Potential tensions:** Aquilonian geopolitical competition. **Opening:** A scholar seeks evidence of a ruined civilization without the patronage of home.

## Cimmerian
**Region:** Hard northern uplands. **Background:** Independent clans, rugged conditions, long contact and conflict with neighbors. **RP traits to explore:** kinship, practical survival, direct speech, distrust of conquest. None is mandatory. **Potential tensions:** Pictish historical feud, Aquilonian borders, Nordheimer raids. **Opening:** A wanderer has no interest in joining a northern war their relatives remember.

## Aesir / Vanir (Nordheimer)
**Region:** The northern lands of Asgard and Vanaheim. **Background:** Northern warrior and settlement traditions with differing tribal allegiances. **RP traits to explore:** oath-taking, sea or land travel, song, trade and family rivalries. **Potential tensions:** Aesir–Vanir rivalry; Cimmerian contact. **Opening:** A Vanir guard and an Aesir merchant reluctantly share watch.

## Hyperborean
**Region:** North/east of key Hyborian realms. **Background:** Severe northern climate and often oppressive political institutions in the stories. **RP traits to explore:** hierarchy, exile, duty, dissent, frontier livelihoods. **Potential tensions:** Aquilonian history and neighboring northern communities, according to era. **Opening:** A deserter wishes the word 'Hyperborean' did not answer every question about them.

## Pict
**Region:** Western wilderness/frontiers. **Background:** Varied tribal and spiritual traditions; stories often focus on contentious Aquilonian settlement frontiers. **RP traits to explore:** hunting, kinship, knowledge of terrain, diplomacy, protective community ties. **Potential tensions:** Aquilonian frontier and ancient Cimmerian myths. **Opening:** A scout has come to trade rather than fulfill someone else's expectation of a raid.

## Shemite
**Region:** Shem's cities, pastures and desert-adjacent communities. **Background:** Distinct communities with mercantile, pastoral and travelling traditions. **RP traits to explore:** poetry, commerce, navigation, mercenary work, spiritual life. **Potential tensions:** historical Stygian control and contact with nearby kingdoms. **Opening:** A caravan musician hopes to establish a permanent shop in the Hub.

## Stygian
**Region:** The kingdom south of the Styx. **Background:** Ancient institutions, temples, elite politics and sorcery in the fiction. **RP traits to explore:** ritual belief, archaeology, merchant wealth, military obligation, dissatisfaction with the state. **Potential tensions:** Shemitish history and exploitation of some southern peoples. **Opening:** A scholar rejects the assumptions others make about Stygian sorcery.

## Darfari
**Region:** Darfar, south of Stygia. **Background:** A fictional people represented through specific Conan tribal/NPC factions. **RP traits to explore:** family, migration, resistance, inherited ritual choices, ordinary survival; avoid treating every Darfari character as a single hostile NPC archetype. **Potential tensions:** Stygian exploitation in some source material. **Opening:** A traveller insists on being known for their deeds rather than a stranger's fear.

## Kushite and southern neighbors
**Region:** Kush and the wider southern lands. **Background:** Distinct cultures, routes and rulers in the Black Kingdoms. **RP traits to explore:** seafaring, settlement, defense, artisan work, diplomacy. **Potential tensions:** vary by polity and era; never infer a monolithic enemy or alliance. **Opening:** A craftsperson joins a mixed caravan and becomes its negotiator.

## Zamorian
**Region:** East of several central Hyborian realms. **Background:** Cities and mobile specialists; fiction often provides opportunities for urban intrigue. **RP traits to explore:** trade, theft, performance, craft, espionage. **Potential tensions:** specific city/clan disputes, not racial hatred. **Opening:** An expert lock worker is tired of every stranger calling them a thief.

## Zingaran
**Region:** Western coastal kingdom. **Background:** Maritime politics, commercial rivalry and sea travel. **RP traits to explore:** sailor, navigator, duelist, trader, harbor official. **Potential tensions:** Argossean maritime competition in settings that support it. **Opening:** A lost ship's captain looks for crew in the Hub.

## Argossean
**Region:** Coastal ports around Argos. **Background:** Trade and shipping connect coastal communities to wider politics. **RP traits to explore:** ship ownership, contracts, imports, tavern operations. **Potential tensions:** port competition or maritime feuds; resolve IC. **Opening:** A broker offers a route into markets blocked by war.

## Hyrkanian / Turanian
**Region:** Eastern steppe and, for Turan, its imperial/city centers. **Background:** Eastern horses, trade, war and shifting geopolitical horizons depending on chronology. **RP traits to explore:** riding, archery, messenger work, commercial ambition, soldiering. **Potential tensions:** western contact is chronologically dependent. **Opening:** A rider has crossed too many borders to believe a map tells the full story.

## Khitan
**Region:** Far-eastern lands. **Background:** Distant travel, specialist arts and distinct philosophical traditions in Conan's expanded world. **RP traits to explore:** crafts, scholarship, journeying, court service. **Potential tensions:** personal or faction-specific; no preassigned common foe. **Opening:** An artisan arrived seeking materials unavailable at home.

## Vendhyan
**Region:** Vendhya and neighboring eastern/southern realms. **Background:** Diverse regional political and courtly settings. **RP traits to explore:** emissary, guard, healer, pilgrim. **Potential tensions:** depend on the chosen exact origin. **Opening:** A former palace messenger would rather be anonymous.

## Himelian
**Region:** Eastern high mountain communities. **Background:** Travel, isolated passes and practical mountaineering. **RP traits to explore:** guide, fighter, hunter, courier. **Potential tensions:** local rivalries in specific mountain settings. **Opening:** A guide offers safe passage in exchange for news of home.

## Yamatai / other expanded cosmetic backgrounds
**Region:** Far eastern-inspired content represented in Conan Exiles cosmetics. **Background:** Always reconcile against the playable lore period and actual enabled content. **RP traits to explore:** craftsmanship, combat practice, long-distance exile. **Potential tensions:** unassigned by SOLACE. **Opening:** A stranger is asked to describe their route west.

## Background application reminder
Write a person, not a census entry. Your character can oppose their homeland, reject a religion, serve another realm or start over entirely. Race/culture relations are for thoughtful RP and have **no mechanical enemy or PvP consequences on their own**.
