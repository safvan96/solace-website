# Frequently Asked Questions

**What kind of server is SOLACE?** 21+ English-language Conan Exiles roleplay with PvP and PvE, an emphasis on player-written stories, social RP and lasting IC consequences.

**Is the Hub safe?** It cannot be raided/sieged, but **PvP is allowed**. A victorious attacker may only injure the target. An attacker who loses falls under NVL: the person they attacked may kill them, take them as a slave, or have them jailed in the Hub if they refuse enslavement. Read `/hub`.

**Do you use dice to settle fights?** No. Mechanical PvP is the default. Specialist RP abilities and appropriate perception/stealth rolls are separate from dice combat.

**Can my friend join a fight after it starts?** No. Narrow exception: the assaulted character reaches their **own base**, and people **already inside** may defend them.

**How do I start PvP?** Intent emote, approved PvP intent command, everyone initially in range declares Join/Surrender and Ready, then a 10-second start timer. See `/rules/pvp`.

**Are raids offline?** No. A qualifying fraction of an active defending clan must be online, a ticket is required and an independent staff member supervises.

**How long may a character be captive?** Captivity lasts **24 hours** for every character, and active captors owe the captive **2 hours of real RP** within it, or the captive is free to leave. Longer-term captivity is allowed when both players agree. Everything happens with consent. See `/rules/consequences`.

**What is Broken?** The Slaver ability has three successful, consensual Breaking stages on the same target. Its full effects are still in development. No roll replaces player consent.

**What are the professions?** Bard, Bounty Hunter, Medic, Fighter, Thief, Alchemist and Slaver. See `/professions`.

**What are the classes?** Sorcerer, Blacksmith and Wise. Each class page lists its abilities level by level. See `/classes`.

**What is the daily/weekly reward?** **10 Silver daily**, **1 Gold weekly**.

**How much is a Feast Ring?** **1 Gold at the Hub.** It has durability. A Blacksmith may craft **one per week**.

**Can I run a market at my base?** Permanent player markets and Market Thespians are Hub-only. Social trade elsewhere isn't an excuse to place a forbidden secondary shop/NPC.

**May I build a second base?** No permanent second base. Small temporary RP camps may be removed after use or extended by permission.

**Can I pay for a higher building limit?** Donation extension was retained as a desired server policy, subject to lawful/platform-permitted implementation. No live product/pricing is published yet.

**When do inactive clan members get removed?** **Three weeks** under the SOLACE amendment; warn staff by ticket if you plan to be away.

**When can I unshelf a living character?** **30 days** after shelving, subject to current conflict/character process.

**Can I use a recording to locate another character?** No, that is metagaming. Evidence stays in tickets; selected promotional clips can be shared only under privacy/content rules.

**Must my culture hate its traditional rival?** No. The lore relationship chart provides historical RP prompts, never forced hostility or KOS rights.

**Is a ticket an automatic RP freeze?** No. Staff can pause an exceptional unsafe/irreversible scene and can review later for a correction.

**Where do I appeal a staff decision?** Use the appropriate appeal/report route listed under `/support` once Discord ticket links are activated.
