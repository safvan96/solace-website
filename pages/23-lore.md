# The Valley at the End of the World
*The lore of SOLACE: what the world is, what you will meet in it, and where your story begins.*

## Built on Conan, made our own
SOLACE is set in the **Exiled Lands of Conan Exiles**, inside Robert E. Howard's **Hyborian Age**: a world of barbarians and dying empires, cruel gods, forbidden sorcery and steel. We keep that world as it is. Its kingdoms, races, gods and history are the ground every character stands on.

On top of it we tell **our own story**: one valley, its people, its laws and the secrets it keeps. Nothing you play has to be read in a book first. You only need a character who arrived here against their will and wants to survive.

| Conan canon (shared by everyone) | SOLACE lore (ours) |
|---|---|
| The Hyborian kingdoms, races and gods | **Sunset Valley**, the settlement where the server lives |
| The Exiled Lands, the wall and the bracelet | Its founding, its **Governor** and the townsfolk you meet |
| The ancient ruins: giant-kings, serpent-men, barrows of the dead | The daily and weekly **hunts** and what the valley does with them |
| Sorcery, its price and its corruption | The current mystery and the seasons our **storytellers** run |

## The World (as your character knows it)
**The Wall.** Every exile remembers the cross. The rope biting into the wrists, the sun on the eyelids, the wind over an ocean of sand. Behind them stands a wall of dark stone older than any kingdom. Nobody has ever seen who guards it. Nobody has ever climbed back over it. The kingdoms of the Hyborian Age send their condemned here because it is cheaper than an executioner and crueller than one: murderers and poets, deserters and princes, the guilty and the merely inconvenient.

**The Bracelet.** Every exile wakes with a band of dull metal locked around the wrist. No clasp, no seam, no maker's mark. Walk too far toward the edge of the world and it begins to burn; walk further and it kills. Nobody agrees on who made it. The old exiles only say that whatever binds you here also keeps something else from finishing you off.

**What came before.** These lands were never empty. Giant-kings once ruled here and their bones still lie in the north. Serpent-men left their faces carved in the ruins and, some say, never truly left the fire beneath the volcano. The barrows of the dead are older than any kingdom, and lately the things inside them do not sleep as well as they used to.

## Sunset Valley
Most exiles die in their first week. Those who live long enough learn that the land kills a lone exile much faster than ten exiles standing back to back.

**Sunset Valley** began as a single fire that nobody put out. Exiles of every race and faith gathered around it and swore that within sight of its light no blade would be drawn on a neighbour. A man who would become the **Governor** turned that oath into law. A stranger who could read the writing on the old stones showed the founders where ancient wells and storerooms lay hidden beneath the grass.

Today the valley has walls, a tavern and inn, a bathhouse, a market, a town hall, a clinic, an arena and a jail. The wine is cheap, the people are kind more often than not, and it is the closest thing to a home the Exiled Lands have to offer.

## What you will meet
### Peoples and tongues
**33 peoples** of the Hyborian world live side by side in the valley, from Aquilonians and Cimmerians to Stygians, Khitans and Picts. Every exile arrives with only two tongues: the one they were born with, and **Trader's Tongue**, the rough speech of every caravan road. The other **26 languages** are learned from other players, one lesson at a time. Speaking another people's language fluently is a respect that cannot be stolen or bought.

### Faiths
**23 faiths** are prayed to in the same streets: Mitra and Set, Crom and Ishtar, Derketo, Ymir, Zath and many more. Some days a prayer is answered with steadier hands and a little bread where there was none. Other days there is only the wind.

### Professions
Every character lives by a trade: **Bard, Bounty Hunter, Medic, Fighter, Thief, Alchemist, Slaver or Courtier**. The rarest walk further: **Sorcerers**, who pay for power with pieces of themselves, the **Wise**, who speak for the gods, and **Blacksmiths**, whose legendary work is worth its weight in gold. Professions make things others need: elixirs, perfumes, salves, lockpicks, scrolls. The valley runs on trade between them.

### Dangers
| Where | What waits there |
|---|---|
| **The Mounds of the Dead** | Barrows older than any kingdom. Things with skulls for faces have begun to walk again, and the creature that leads them carries **Green Gems** in its chest. *(Daily Hunt)* |
| **The Abandoned Silvermine** | The miners fled years ago. What crawled up from the lower shafts did not. Its guardians carry **Blue Gems**. *(Weekly Hunt)* |
| **The wilds** | Sand, heat, beasts and storms. The bracelet decides how far you may run. |
| **The volcano road** | Caravan drivers on this road do not stop after dark, and will not tell you why. |
| **Other exiles** | Not everyone kept the oath of the fire. Fights follow the server's PvP intent and consequence rules: the winner may give the loser one Injury or let them go. |
| **Sorcery** | Real, rare and expensive. It is granted by staff through roleplay, never picked from a list. |

### Travel and work
Caravans run from Sunset Valley to **eight stops** across the Exiled Lands. The town hires hands for **guard duty and tavern work**, the scholar pays for the **hunts**, and the arena, clinic, bathhouse and auction call for players when they need them.

## The Missing Governor
And yet something is wrong in the valley.

The Governor has not been seen for days. His office is quiet and his chair is empty. His scholar keeps the ledgers and the hunts running as if nothing has changed, and tells everyone the Governor is travelling.

The flower seller says he looked tired. The tavern keeper says he drank more than usual. The man who empties the chamberpots says nothing at all, unless you ask him the right question.

## Your story begins
Every new exile starts with the story quest **Where Am I?** Find the Governor's scholar in Sunset Valley and ask him the only question that matters to a newcomer. The quest takes you through the town: you will meet its people, help them with small tasks, learn what each profession does, earn useful items along the way, and find out what this place really is.

After that, the valley is yours. Our **storytellers** run seasons and events that grow out of this story, and what players do in them becomes part of the lore.

*New to the Conan world? You don't need to read anything first. Pick a people, a faith and a trade, and let the valley teach you the rest.*
