# Peoples & Cultures of the Hyborian World
*Your birthplace gives you a history, not a mandatory personality.*

SOLACE is based on Conan lore. In Conan, many options commonly called 'races' in game menus are better described as peoples, realms or cultural backgrounds. Do not assume a person's background decides every alliance or that a historical feud gives you permission to KOS someone. Any custom racial stat bonuses, application restrictions, exceptional species, culture-specific abilities or quotas must be published separately before they exist.

## Choose a background
These are brief writing prompts, not exhaustive cultural laws, and each culture can support many character types.

| People/culture | Starting point for your character | Interesting RP hooks |
|---|---|---|
| **Aquilonian** | Western Hyborian kingdom with nobility, soldiers and frontier politics. | Soldier, courtier, settler, critic of the crown. |
| **Nemedian** | Powerful Hyborian kingdom with scholarly and religious traditions. | Archivist, diplomat, rival noble, explorer. |
| **Cimmerian** | Rugged highland clans north of Aquilonia; Conan's people. | Hunter, warrior, clan exile, wanderer. |
| **Nordheimer** | Northern **Aesir** and **Vanir** peoples, with distinct loyalties and traditions. | Raider, skald, seafarer, mercenary. |
| **Hyperborean** | Cold eastern/northern kingdom, known in stories for stern power structures. | Escapee, soldier, traveller, political dissident. |
| **Pict** | Tribal peoples of the western wilderness with longstanding frontier conflicts. | Scout, ritualist, trader, clan representative. |
| **Shemite** | Diverse Shemitish cities, pastoral communities and desert traditions. | Merchant, caravan guard, performer, nomad. |
| **Stygian** | Ancient southern kingdom associated with mystery, temples and the Styx. | Scholar, sorcery seeker, soldier, exile. |
| **Darfari** | A people of Darfar, south of Stygia; Conan fiction features distinctive religious/tribal groups. | Traveller, outsider, rebel, devotee; avoid reducing a person to an NPC enemy caricature. |
| **Kushite** | Southern peoples from Kush and neighboring regions. | Sailor, artisan, warrior, emissary. |
| **Zamorian** | Eastern urban cultures, frequently associated in stories with trade and intrigue. | Trader, thief, court agent, exile. |
| **Zingaran** | Western maritime kingdom with political rivals and seafaring interests. | Sailor, corsair, merchant, noble. |
| **Argossean** | Port-city and coastal commercial culture. | Shipmaster, broker, harbor worker. |
| **Hyrkanian** | Horse-oriented steppe and eastern traditions. | Rider, scout, caravan escort. |
| **Turanian** | Eastern imperial culture associated with military expansion and wealthy cities. | Officer, trader, courier. |
| **Khitan** | Far-eastern kingdom/cultural sphere with distant philosophies and arts. | Wanderer, artisan, scholar. |
| **Vendhyan** | Far-eastern/southern courtly and regional traditions. | Envoy, guard, healer, exile. |
| **Himelian** | Mountain societies of the eastern highlands. | Guide, scout, herder. |
| **Yamatai** | Eastern-inspired culture present in Conan Exiles's expanded cosmetic setting. | Swordsperson, craftsperson, distant traveller. |
| **Hyborian (general)** | A broad cultural ancestry, not one single automatic kingdom identity. | Choose a more specific city, polity or frontier background where useful. |
| **Other Conan backgrounds** | Kambujan, Khari, tribal/region-specific characters and other lore-consistent origins. | Ask the lore team when a less common option needs clarification. |

**Availability warning:** Listing a people here is a lore guide, not an automatic confirmation that every cosmetic pack, mod race, supernatural identity or homebrew ability is playable at launch. The final playable list must match the actual character-creation implementation.

## Historical relationships: a starting point for RP
**Legend:** `Rivalry/Border conflict` = historical state/tribal tension; `Raids/hostility` = recurring conflict in published source material; `Trade/complex` = contact without a single permanent alignment; `Internal differences` = a grouped category contains rival subgroups. **None is an automatic character-specific enemy tag, combat permission, racial bonus, or fixed alliance.** Canon chronology varies, and SOLACE has not settled an exact lore year.

| Pair / grouping | Typical historical tension or relationship | Why an encounter might matter | Gameplay status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aquilonia ↔ Nemedia | Rival great Hyborian powers; political and military competition. | Border officer meets a Nemedian merchant or envoy. | Historical context, **not** automatic hostility. |
| Aquilonia ↔ Pictish frontier groups | Frontier conflict and raiding are recurring Conan themes. | Patrol, captive exchange, illicit border trade. | Historical context, **not** KOS. |
| Aquilonia ↔ Cimmerian clans | Northern frontier contact, raids and attempted expansion create distrust. | Soldier and highlander disagree over a frontier claim. | Character choice decides the response. |
| Cimmerians ↔ Picts | Long mythic/historical antagonism in Hyborian history. | Old oral tradition colors a new meeting. | No inherited personal blood feud by default. |
| Aesir ↔ Vanir | Distinct Nordheimer groups often depicted in mutual conflict. | Competing northern warbands or unexpected cooperation. | Internal northern rivalry, not universal enmity. |
| Nordheimer ↔ Cimmerian clans | Raids and border contact recur in northern tales. | Shared survival against a third faction. | Situation-dependent. |
| Stygia ↔ Shemitish peoples | Long history of domination, trade, power struggles and shifting independence. | Priest, trader and runaway meet in exile. | Historical context, not a forced relationship. |
| Stygia ↔ Darfari/other southern neighbors | Cross-border coercion and slaving appear in Conan's world. | A former captive seeks allies or a new life. | This is a state/history prompt, never consent to enslavement. |
| Aquilonia ↔ Hyperborea | Episodes of hostility in Hyborian geopolitical history. | Rival claimants meet far from home. | Era-sensitive, not permanent faction war. |
| Zingara ↔ Argos | Neighboring maritime commerce and competition. | Merchant/corsair rivalry at a neutral market. | RP hook rather than declared war. |
| Hyrkanian/Turanian powers ↔ western kingdoms | Contact and expansion change dramatically across the Hyborian chronology. | Courier, scout or displaced noble. | Requires chosen setting year. |

### How to use this table
Play a believable personal history. A Cimmerian and an Aquilonian may be old enemies, comrades, lovers or strangers. An inherited grievance can start a scene, but only IC actions and actual approved combat rules can escalate it. Do not use a website culture table as a metagaming hit list.

## Character application prompts
Where were you born? Who raised you? What does your character believe about home? Which language, faith or profession matters to them? What is one stereotype they fit and one they don't? Why did exile change their plans? How much of their homeland's politics do they actually know?
