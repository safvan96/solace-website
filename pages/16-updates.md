# News & Rule Changes
## Draft v0.1: 30 September 2026
SOLACE's first website content pack combines the adopted mechanical baseline with approved SOLACE decisions and the September planning meetings. **It is a drafting package, not a claim that hosting, mods, application forms or the final rulebook have launched.**

### Confirmed decisions documented here
- Eight professions (Courtier added); three classes (Wise added).
- Mechanical PvP; new no-late-join rule with a narrow home-base defense exception.
- Hub permits PvP but not raids; an initiating attacker who loses falls under NVL (the victim may kill, enslave or jail them in the Hub).
- 24-hour captivity with two hours' required active captor RP; consent form to be published.
- Three-week clan inactivity threshold; 30-day living-character unshelving.
- 10 Silver daily / 1 Gold weekly.
- Feast Ring durability, 1 Gold Hub price, one Blacksmith craft per week.
- Conan lore; original building caps adopted, player markets restricted to Hub.

### Before publication
Resolve pending fields in `reference/OPEN_DECISIONS.md`, run content review and technical verification, then publish a dated rule revision with staff/owner sign-off. Changes must state their effective date, whether they affect ongoing scenes, and the appeal/exception process.
