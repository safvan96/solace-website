# Dice Effects & Statuses
*Which statuses change your rolls, and for how long.*

## How dice effects work
Many skills leave a lasting effect on the character they touch. While the effect lasts, the listed skills are raised or lowered automatically on every roll. The effect is shown as a status above the character and in their character sheet. It ends when its time runs out or when it is removed (for example by Wise III: Purification or by treating an injury).

- Effects from rolled skills apply **only on a success**.
- Skills that affect everyone nearby (Performance, Liar's Perfume) roll automatically for each character in range: the character's **Insight** against the caster's skill. Those who lose the roll receive the effect.
- A penalty applies to **all** of that character's rolls with the listed skill, not only against the caster.

## Real in-game bonuses
These change your actual Health, Stamina and damage while the effect lasts.

| Skill | Health | Stamina | Weapon damage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bard: Inspire | +60 max Health (and healed by 60) | – | – |
| Medic: Revitalize | +20 max Health (and healed by 20) | +20 max Stamina (and refilled by 20) | – |
| Fighter: Endurance | – | +10 max Stamina | – |
| Sorcerer V: Empowerment | +20 max Health | +20 max Stamina | +5% Strength · +5% Agility |
| Wise V: Divine Empowerment | +20 max Health | +20 max Stamina | +5% Strength · +5% Agility |

**Bonuses do not stack.** Health and Stamina bonuses of the same kind do not add up; the highest one counts. A character who is Inspired (+60) and then Revitalized keeps +60 Health and additionally gains Revitalize's +20 Stamina and its dice bonuses. Sorcerer V and Wise V weapon-damage bonuses do not stack with each other either. When an effect ends, the maximum returns to normal.
