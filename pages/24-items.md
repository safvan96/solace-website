## Blacksmith fittings
Applied to a weapon or armour like a kit; the upgrade is permanent. Made by Blacksmiths (Hub price: 1 Gold each).

| Icon | Fitting | Effect |
|---|---|---|

## Jewels
Jewels strengthen the one who carries them. There are **four kinds**, each in **three tiers**. You can carry **one jewel of each kind** at the same time: one Strength, one Agility, one Health and one Stamina jewel, of any tier. Sold for Gold.

| Jewel | Bonus | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strength (red) | Strength | ![Jewel Strength T1](img/items/Jewel_Strength_T1.png) | ![Jewel Strength T2](img/items/Jewel_Strength_T2.png) | ![Jewel Strength T3](img/items/Jewel_Strength_T3.png) |
