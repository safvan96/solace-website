# Classes
*Specializations you develop through play.*

Classes are different from Professions. They open through applications, quests and mechanics rather than simply describing a character's everyday work. **Three classes are planned: Sorcerer, Blacksmith and Wise.** Mechanical PvP stays mechanical; custom class checks are narrative/specialist tools and cannot replace combat results.

# Sorcerer
*Knowledge has a price. Progress is earned through blood rites.*

The Sorcerer is a lore-conscious custom class requiring an application and IC ritual development. The meeting favored five levels with a **fresh blood requirement at each new stage**, rather than a cumulative bank. It also explored each ability below. Levels IV–V's blood counts were inferred from the progression pattern rather than unambiguously repeated in the transcript, so verify before launch.

| Tier | Meeting-derived blood requirement | Proposed unlocked power | What it is meant to do | Publication state |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| I | 1 character's blood | **Intent Reading** | Sense broadly positive/negative intent directed at the Sorcerer. | Proposed |
| II | 2 characters' blood | **Mind Control** | Attempt a bounded mental/behavioral command. | Limits/consent/counterplay pending |
| III | 3 characters' blood | **Memory Erasure** | Affect a particular target's memory of an incident, not what others know. | Limits/consent pending |
| IV | 4 character blood sources *(inferred)* | **Invisibility** | Around **15 minutes** of limited invisibility under agreed detection/combat rules. | Duration discussed; implementation pending |
| V | 5 character blood sources *(inferred)* | **Empowerment** | An ultimate buff for self or one eligible nearby character. | Exact values pending |

### Empowerment: meeting's unfinalized candidate
A **2-hour** buff once per day was discussed, with approximate **+5% Strength**, **+5% Agility**, **+20 Stamina** and **+20 Health**. Transcript uncertainty around STR notation and stacking makes these **not live balance values**. Sorcery branches such as Necromancer, Demonologist, Shaman, Hexer and Diviner were floated as concepts, not approved subclasses. No automatic first-contact PK right is granted to Bounty Hunters based on suspected sorcery; this was only debated. Ritual proof, blood sourcing, cooldown, staff ticket and opt-outs all require implementation specifications.

# Blacksmith
*The person who keeps rare equipment in circulation.*

Blacksmith is a **Class**, not a Profession. It uses quests and work to progress towards specialist production and repair services. Regular characters are not forbidden from making basic gear; Blacksmith specializes in exceptional crafting.

### Confirmed special catalog
- **Master Weapon Fitting**.
- **Agony-like special items** (exact item IDs/variants to be confirmed).
- **Feast Ring**: an item that grants the intended feast effect, and **has durability**.

### Feast Ring facts
| Property | SOLACE decision |
|---|---|
| Hub purchase price | **1 Gold** |
| Blacksmith production limit | **One Feast Ring per Blacksmith per week** |
| Durability | **Yes**; precise points/wear rate TBD |
| Blacksmith resale price | Negotiated between players, unless later specified |
| Repair method | Specialist Repair Kit idea discussed; exact recipe and economics not settled |

The meeting also considered a **three-stage** Blacksmith progression through quests/boss work, **4–5-day** stage cooldowns, craftable Repair Kits and other fittings/enchantments. Their exact rules are not yet owner-confirmed. The **one-per-week confirmed quota applies expressly to the Feast Ring**; do not silently extend it to every Master Weapon Fitting or Agony-like item.

### Why it matters
A Fighter needs equipment maintained. A trader brings rare materials. A Blacksmith can become the most useful person in the village without being the strongest warrior. Read `/economy` for daily and weekly currency and the Hub vendor route.

# Wise
*Faith, knowledge and divine ritual.*

The Wise are scholars, spiritual leaders, priests, mystics and keepers of divine knowledge. Unlike Sorcerers, who gain power through forbidden practices, blood rituals and manipulation, the Wise gain their abilities through faith, knowledge, worship and divine ritual.

A Wise character must follow the lore and religious practices of the deity they worship. A ritual dedicated to Mitra should not look the same as a ritual dedicated to Set, Ymir, Derketo or another deity. Each ritual must reflect the beliefs, traditions, offerings, prayers and practices of that god.

### Wise ritual rules
Every progression ritual must follow these rules:
- A ticket must be opened before the ritual.
- The ritual must be recorded.
- The ritual must be lore-appropriate.
- The required number of worshippers must take part.
- All required participants must worship the same deity.
- A successful ritual roll is required.
- If the ritual fails, the progression or effect is not granted.
- Staff may review the ritual if necessary.
- Rituals may require offerings, prayers, ceremonies or other religious acts, depending on the deity.
