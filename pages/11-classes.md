# Classes
*Specializations you develop through play.*

Every class grants **+2 to two RPR skills**. Class abilities that need a roll use **1d20 + skill**; see `/rpr` for the full dice system.

Classes are different from Professions. They open through applications, quests and mechanics rather than simply describing a character's everyday work. **Three classes are planned: Sorcerer, Blacksmith and Wise.** A character with a Class must also choose a Profession. Mechanical PvP stays mechanical; custom class checks are narrative/specialist tools and cannot replace combat results.

# Sorcerer
*Knowledge has a price. Progress is earned through blood rites.*

The Sorcerer is a lore-conscious custom class requiring an application and IC ritual development. The meeting favored five levels with a **fresh blood requirement at each new stage**, rather than a cumulative bank. It also explored each ability below.

| Tier | Blood requirement | Unlocked power | What it does |
|---:|---|---|---|
| I | 1 character's blood | **Intent Reading** | Sense broadly positive/negative intent directed at the Sorcerer. |
| II | 2 characters' blood | **Mind Control** | The target carries out one single command. |
| III | 3 characters' blood | **Memory Erasure** | Affect a particular target's memory of an incident, not what others know. |
| IV | 4 characters' blood | **Invisibility** | **30 minutes** of limited invisibility. |
| V | 5 characters' blood | **Empowerment** | An ultimate buff for self or one nearby character. |

### Empowerment: meeting's unfinalized candidate
A **2-hour** buff once per day was discussed, with approximate **+5% Strength**, **+5% Agility**, **+20 Stamina** and **+20 Health**. Transcript uncertainty around STR notation and stacking makes these **not live balance values**. Sorcery branches such as Necromancer, Demonologist, Shaman, Hexer and Diviner were floated as concepts, not approved subclasses. No automatic first-contact PK right is granted to Bounty Hunters based on suspected sorcery; this was only debated. Ritual proof, blood sourcing, cooldown, staff ticket and opt-outs all require implementation specifications.

# Blacksmith
*The person who keeps rare equipment in circulation.*


The Blacksmith crafts legendary fittings and the Feast Ring. No dice roll is needed; required materials are shown in-game. Every item sells for **1 Gold** at the Hub market.

| Item | Effect | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| **Legendary Weapon Fitting** | Increases Weapon Damage and Armor Penetration. | 2 days |
| **Legendary Spike Weapon Fitting** | Increases Armor Penetration. | 2 days |
| **Legendary Armor Fitting** | Increases Armor Points. | 2 days |
| **Feast Ring** | Carry up to 1,000 Feast items with no weight. 1,000 uses, then it breaks. | 1 week |

# Wise
*Faith, knowledge and divine ritual.*

The Wise are scholars, spiritual leaders, priests, mystics and keepers of divine knowledge. Unlike Sorcerers, who gain power through forbidden practices, blood rituals and manipulation, the Wise gain their abilities through faith, knowledge, worship and divine ritual.

A Wise character must follow the lore and religious practices of the deity they worship. A ritual dedicated to Mitra should not look the same as a ritual dedicated to Set, Ymir, Derketo or another deity. Each ritual must reflect the beliefs, traditions, offerings, prayers and practices of that god.

### Wise ritual rules
Every new Wise level needs its own ritual. No blood is required.
- A ticket must be opened before the ritual. The required ritual materials are shared in the ticket.
- The ritual must be lore-appropriate for the Wise's deity.
- The ritual RP must be recorded and sent to staff through the ticket.
- The required number of worshippers must take part.
- Once the ritual is approved, the related skill unlocks.

## Class application
Classes are special applications. You must request the Class in a ticket and clearly describe the kind of RP you want to do with it.

**A character with a Class must also choose a Profession.**
