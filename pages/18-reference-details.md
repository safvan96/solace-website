# Reference Details & Edge Cases
*The short pages explain what you need most often. This annex collects inherited edge-case mechanics and their exceptions so no one has to guess during a scene.*

> **Release note:** This is a detailed inherited-rule annex, subject to successful implementation of the actual Conan mod setup. A source-specific item, racial feat or portal that SOLACE does not install must not be treated as available merely because the original reference mentions it. Owner-approved SOLACE overrides take precedence in case of conflict.

## Post-combat specifics
- A downed character is **Slightly Injured** at a minimum and stays at the down location while mechanical PvP is unresolved. Do not revive or carry the character out until fighting has ended, except for a permitted mechanical return to the exact location of the body.
- After fighting, the winning side may permit aided movement. Players use a suitable aid/accept-aid emote, walk rather than run, and cannot warp until **three map tiles** from the original down point under the inherited rule.
- If a new valid incident interrupts consequence processing, a person already downed can only surrender, not join or run. Any applicable unattended timer pauses during the interruption. This must **not** be exploited as a loophole to bring late allies into the original battle.
- No unauthorised healing from outside the winning side during consequence processing. Moderate-or-higher injury blocks abilities/spells unless the explicit ability allows limited self-healing.
- Only participants in the original legal encounter may give its consequences. You cannot hand a captured target to an uninvolved third party solely to deliver an otherwise unavailable consequence. A truly separate later event requires its own lawful initiation and reviewer scrutiny.

## Injury and bodily consequence categories
- **Major dismemberment:** removal of a limb or comparable permanent organ loss; source baseline says this is a **second-down** outcome and cannot naturally regenerate. Restoration requires a valid healing mechanic; the character's gear use must reflect loss (e.g. one-handed weapons after loss of arm/hand). Genital injury is sensitive content and requires specific OOC permission.
- **Mutilation:** long-term scar/brand or loss of minor part, with Moderate Injury under the inherited scheme; special severe vision loss is treated as a later/second-down outcome. Inherited magical treatment may remove it only through a matching approved item/spell. Consent Form governs how far any lasting change may go.
- **Robbery:** use exactly one lawful table choice for its category and obey the special crafted-item limits. A normal consequence is never blanket permission to loot buildings. A clue used for scrying may be emote-only, without taking a protected physical object.
- **Spells:** the source's Primary/Secondary consequence spell design needs a published Conan-specific roster before it is functional.
- **Permadeath:** normally a considered, administratively reviewed outcome. If it is permitted, it is the **only** consequence on that character. A major attack on a leader is not a free-kill pass on the entire settlement.
- **Sexual assault content:** requires explicit, specific OOC agreement to participate in any sensitive theme, and refusal is sufficient. No prior IC offense creates automatic permission. No explicit scene is required by this website.

## PvP intent edge cases
- Initial observers in **local range** must declare their response at the start; this is different from bringing someone in once combat is underway.
- Leaving an instance through its normal exit is permitted, but neither side may exploit loading screens or heal immediately at the doorway. Move **5 foundations** before fighting outside.
- Source includes a Khari Dungeon-specific escape shortcut that counts as surrender. **Do not activate this rule unless the exact location exists on SOLACE's deployed map and the path has been tested.**
- Flight is not an invulnerability loophole; if approved flight mechanics exist, land before relevant conflict.
- Event KOS is only for explicitly announced, status-tagged activities; source used Lake Town Heist/Relic Hunting, **not current SOLACE event names**. Staff must publish the present event flag and player identification before enabling.

## Raid edge cases
- Thief lockpicking allows **at most two entrants** before a full approved raid is necessary; do not open additional doors for an entire unregistered army.
- Raid objective may be broadly described but cannot be omitted.
- Defenders may make the permitted home warp to protect their base; attackers do not gain comparable entry warp.
- Attackers who fail to arrive from the declared location within **10 minutes** after the raid start timer may lose the raid and receive **24-hour** cooldown as a source administrative consequence.
- A declared attempt ordinarily creates **3-day** target-group cooldown upon completion, with defender counter-action and defender-flight distinctions set out in `/rules/raids`.
- Spectators who are not actually involved cannot arrive purely to watch OOC; allies require real IC communication rather than Discord mobilization.
- Source requires music boxes off during raids, and prevents post-declaration building/dismantling/repair outside the identified siege-engine exception.

## Looking for RP (LFRP), if the relevant mod is installed
- Use a clear **Conflict/Hostile** tag where an LFRP advertisement is intended to lead to antagonistic roleplay (especially for Slaver scenarios).
- Disable LFRP immediately if a scene becomes conflict or when another participant legitimately requests it; keep disabled through consequences/captivity.
- Never use a live LFRP tag to locate someone's hidden base while approaching/exploring without consent.
- Only an assigned Dungeon Master may apply a DM tag.

## Character/account edge cases
- Do not create a new character to pursue vengeance about your own previous character's death. No involvement in one's own old-character resurrection process.
- A reroll contest must be about unresolved recent conflict and may be declined if there is no actual pursuit/engagement. Only approved reviewers may decide its merits.
- Standard shelves retain character progression where technically possible; rare/special background entitlement from a retired ruleset is not automatically transferred.
- Leaving Discord: open a notice ticket. A fake or alternate account used to evade bans may cause action against linked accounts where a knowingly involved participant helps evade enforcement.
