# Privacy, Recording & Sharing
*Keep the memory. Protect the people in it.*

## The baseline
Do not record/stream a current RP scene to gather IC information unavailable to your character. Do not use another person's stream, screenshots or a recording to hunt them, follow secret negotiations or rally OOC allies. This is metagaming even when the media was posted publicly elsewhere.

## Confidential evidence
Recordings and screenshots may be submitted to staff when relevant to a rule violation or exploit. Keep them inside the ticket and do not distribute them to players who were not involved. Avoid selective editing that hides important context; mark time stamps where possible.

## Approved community promotion
SOLACE may allow screenshots, polished highlights, event footage and cinematic promotional media that depict **consenting participants** and do not expose restricted scenes or create IC advantage. Agree to publication with the people identifiable in a recording before posting it. When in doubt, request a content-sharing review ticket. Prior-season footage does not automatically waive a person's privacy.

## Sensitive content
Never post explicit personal/sexual scenes, personal information, staff tickets, private Discord conversation content or identifying details without appropriate permission. Avoid doxxing and unwanted contact. A consent form covers roleplay boundaries, not blanket rights to publicize material.

## Website implementation notes
Privacy notice must accurately disclose the actual analytics, cookies, embedded Discord widgets, hosting and user forms once chosen. Do **not** invent GDPR claims, a company address, a retention period or data-contact email. Add a separate final legal policy before collecting personal data through the website.
